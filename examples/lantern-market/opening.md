The fog comes up the canal like a slow grey tide, and then, all at once, the Lily Raft is full of light.

Stalls unfold from nothing. Awnings snap open. Lanterns blink awake one after another along the floating square: amber, rose, a green so pale it is almost silver. Somewhere a bell rings three times and the crowd that has been waiting on the bridges pours onto the boards, which sway gently under so many feet.

You are not here to shop. The licence in your coat pocket is still stiff and new: **FINDER, by order of the Market Warden.** Your own lantern hangs from your belt, cold and dark. It will light, the Warden told you, when the market decides it trusts you.

Halfway along the first row, a lantern goes out.

Not blown out. It simply *dims*, like a held breath let go, until the glass is black. The stall-keeper beneath it, a small grey figure wrapped in folded wings, stares up at it and then at the faces around them, blinking, as if trying to remember who everyone is.

A hand lands on your shoulder.

[[Ottilie Brask/stern]]
"That's the fourth this week," says the Warden, not taking her eyes off the dark lantern. Her brass spectacles catch the light of the ones still burning. "And you, Finder, have just found your first job."
