# Changelog

## 1.8.1
**Cheaper memory for long stories, species pictures, dice difficulty, LoRA trigger words and a tidier app**

- **Adult pictures with a doubled head are redrawn.** A reference-picture adult maker in ComfyUI sometimes draws the character twice (two heads). The app now counts the faces in a finished adult picture and, if it finds two or more, redraws it with another seed (up to twice). This needs the InsightFace face detector in ComfyUI's own Python (the one the face-likeness workflow already uses); when it is not there, nothing is checked and nothing changes.
- **Z-Image Turbo picture models.** Z-Image (Turbo) checkpoints are recognised by what they are and get their own ComfyUI workflow (8 steps, no negative prompt, sentence-style prompts; no reference pictures).
- **Cheaper memory for long stories (100+ turns).** Four changes that cut what each turn costs and keep the story's details from getting lost:
  - **One-line digests.** After every reply the small helper AI (the one that already logs key events, so no extra call) also writes one line about the exchange: where, who, what changed, promises, items. The memory summary is now written from these lines instead of the full replies, so updating it reads a fraction as much. Turn it off in Settings → Memory & cost → Turn digests. Stories played before this can get digests for their older turns under 🧠 Memory → *One-line digests of older turns*.
  - **Older events come back when they matter.** Each turn the app searches the digests for older events that share names, places or items with your move and the last reply, and gives the narrator up to five as a short reminder. Because those details can be fetched again, the shared summary can stay about a third smaller.
  - **The picture list is sent once.** The long list of which characters have which mood pictures (and places and outfits) used to be re-sent in full with every move. It now sits in the cached part of the prompt, and only newly drawn pictures are added at the end of a move. It is refreshed at the moments the cache is rewritten anyway.
  - **Half-price batch for background work.** Writing digests for an old story is done with Claude's batch service (half the price; takes a few minutes instead of seconds). The Usage page counts batch calls at half price.
- **Species can have several reference pictures.** On a species card (🖼 Art → Species & makers…) you can now add a **general look**, a **male** and a **female** picture, plus **daily clothing** and **combat clothing**. A species of one sex (set with *Gender of this species*) shows just that one sex. Each picture can be uploaded, copied from one of your characters, or drawn (pick one of 4). A new character is drawn from tiny copies of the picture of their sex and of the clothing, so they share the species' look and outfit but keep their own face; fight pictures use the combat clothing picture, and a fight picture of a character who already has pictures gets it too. The species library, story packs and the character page carry all of them. The *Draw* button for the general, male, female and daily pictures now always draws in everyday clothes (it used to draw in combat clothes whenever combat clothing was filled in).
- **Fixed: hand edits to the side panel no longer get overridden on the next turn.** The narrator kept printing the old value from its earlier status lines and the app took it back. Now the narrator is told about your correction, an old value it repeats is ignored (and corrected in the printed status line), and it can still change the value when the story really does. The edit stops being protected once the narrator prints your value, writes a different one, or after 10 turns.
- **Fixed: the "Edit the side panel" window could not scroll.** With many fields or characters the lower part was cut off and unreachable. It now scrolls, with Save and Cancel always visible.
- **Dice now have a game difficulty.** Choose how much your stats are worth on a roll, in 📝 Story → Story → Options (which now holds all the dice settings: automatic rolls, game difficulty and the usual roll difficulty; the ⚡ 🎲 Check… button stays a plain "roll on what I typed") or in the one-time dice question for a new game-like story: **Easy** (everyday life: stats 1 to 10, 5 is an ordinary person, 1 is very hard to pass, 10 almost never fails), **Normal** (tabletop style: 10 is average, +1 per 2 points) or **Hard** (a stat of 5 is average, the way dice worked before; stories keep this unless changed). The question suggests one from your starting stats. Story packages carry the choice. Letter-grade stats (F to SSS) are scaled to fit.
- **LoRAs now get their trigger words.** A LoRA often does nothing unless its trigger words are in the prompt. When you add a LoRA (a checkpoint's LoRA list, the adult picture maker, the video settings) or pick one for a character's face or a species, the app reads its trigger words from the LoRA file and fills them in (`name @ 0.8 | trigger words`, editable, `| none` if it needs none). Every picture drawn with that LoRA gets the words first in the prompt (video LoRAs get them in the motion prompt), without repeating words that are already there. Face and species LoRAs with an empty trigger box use the words from the file too.
- **The Extended Full Set is now part of Expressions.** The third "Extended Full Set" option is gone from every Draw menu and from the picture-type editor. Its 40 expressions (laugh, grin, cry, sob, awe, contempt, flustered, panic, tease, kiss, fake smile and the rest) now belong to the **Expressions** image category, so *All: Expressions* draws them together with the Basic and Extended faces. **Wedding dress** is an outfit idea instead of a picture: in a character's Outfits panel (switch Outfits on in Art → Style & options) press *＋ wedding dress*, then draw any expression in it. The near-duplicate "sulk" was folded into "sulky", and one rarely used expression is no longer a built-in type. Pictures you already have of these keep working: wedding-dress pictures moved into that outfit and sulk pictures became sulky.
- **Animate all your pictures.** New 🎬 Animate all buttons make a video of every picture of a character (their Images tab) or of the whole story (🖼 Art → 🎞 Videos), one after another, skipping pictures that already have one. And a new setting, “Animate every new picture automatically” (🖼 Art → Picture makers → Video generation), turns each newly drawn picture into a video in the background. New pictures are always drawn first, so the story never waits on a video, and ⏹ Stop pictures cancels the queued videos too. Needs a local ComfyUI video setup. In stories with adult content on, adult pictures (adult picture types such as positions and finishes, explicit prompts, scene and “right now” pictures drawn as adult) automatically use your Adult (NSFW) video workflow, model and LoRAs, with the matching motion; everyday pictures use the everyday ones. You can also choose which kinds to animate: pressing 🎬 Animate all opens a list with a tick box for each group (Expressions, Actions, Positions, Finishes, Other adult, any picture types you added, outfit pictures, characters at places, place pictures, scene pictures, the "right now" picture and the cover), each with its picture count and a live total, so you can animate only the expressions, only the adult pictures, or only the places.
- **Tidier, more consistent screens (full app check).** Every menu, window and button was opened and tested. Changes:
  - The narrator and picture drop-downs no longer list music, video, robot or agent models (Lyria, Grok video, Robotics, Antigravity…); picture lists no longer offer video models; Nano Banana moved to the picture side.
  - The suggested picture maker for a new computer is your chosen default (or a general model such as FLUX.2 klein), not the first file in alphabetical order.
  - A character's Images tab: each picture's name sits on its own line with its buttons in one neat row; small icon buttons are grey (only × delete is red); a proper “📁 Upload my own…” button replaces the browser's file box; “Delete all their pictures” moved to the bottom, away from the Draw buttons.
  - Window titles all carry their icon (🎭 Cast & gallery, 🧑 Avatar, 🔍 Search, 📍 Places, 💲 Usage & cost, 🗂 Storage, 💾 Backups, 🏁 Possible endings, 🔊 Read-aloud voices, ⚙ Settings).
  - Help texts and error messages that pointed to old menu names (“Settings → AI & story”, “Settings → Pictures”, “Story & narration”…) now point to where things really are.
  - The Home button “📦 Import a story” is now “📦 Open a story package”, so it is not confused with “📥 Import a save”.
  - Creativity, Longest reply and Context size in a story's ⚙ Writing & panel tab now say they apply to all stories.
  - With ⚡ Quick instructions and 💡 suggested replies both open, those panels scroll inside themselves so the story keeps most of the screen.
  - Phone menu: every header button shows its name (Tools, Panel, Home, Settings).
  - The “⏹ Stop pictures” button now floats just above the typing box instead of covering the Send button (especially on phones), and moves up when the quick bar or suggested replies open.
  - Places pictures are shown smaller in the Places window (click one to see it full size).
  - “Export as text” no longer contains picture codes or hidden lines.
  - A picture file that cannot be read no longer causes an error; the original is shown instead of a thumbnail.
  - The browser tab has an icon (📖).
- **A generic character who gets a name now gets their own picture.** When the story names a generic stand-in (the waitress turns out to be Mara), Mara becomes a new character with her own card and her own picture request, using the narrator's fuller description, instead of reusing the generic character's picture. The narrator is told to describe them in more detail the first time they are named.
- **Outfit and place pictures can be copied too.** The ⧉ button is now also on a character's outfit pictures and place pictures: save one as another expression or action, or as another outfit's version of a picture.

## 1.3.0
**New**
- **🖼 Art menu: every picture tool in one place.** An overview of each kind of picture with its status (characters, your avatar, Places, Backgrounds, cover, intro panels, scene pictures, picture makers), each opening its tool, with a **← Art menu** button on every screen to come back. *Style & options* holds the art direction, setting and every picture switch; *Backgrounds* holds the backdrop tools; *Picture makers* holds the picture services (moved here from Settings → Pictures; also reachable from the Home screen). ⭳ is now **Export & backups** (text, illustrated page, story package, this story's backups, whole-app backup).
- **Character memory (Settings → Memory & cost, on by default).** Each character's key events live on their card and are sent to the narrator only while they are in the scene, in a short note on your newest message (size: Small, Medium or Large). The shared memory summary stays short (plot, places, items, open threads). The helper AI condenses a long log by itself (your own entries are never touched), and an old summary's "Key characters" part moves onto the cards the first time it is updated (a backup is saved first).
- **Append-only memory summary.** A memory update writes only the new entries and a refreshed status paragraph and the app appends them, instead of rewriting the whole summary. On a 70-reply story: about half the input and an eighth of the output tokens of a rewrite, and 5 seconds instead of 23. If the model's answer does not fit, the old full rewrite is used.
- **Small helpers (optional second helper model).** Pick a cheaper or local model for the short jobs after every reply: key events, new characters, side panel, the background place check, suggested replies and picture prompts. Default: the same as Recaps & helpers.
- **Places:** fixed pictures of rooms the story keeps returning to (name, describe, draw 4 options, pick). The narrator shows one with a tag when a scene starts there.
- **Scene pictures most turns (option)**, and a choice of where scene pictures show: under the reply, a wide **banner above or below the narration**, or in the side panel's Right now card.
- **Right now card (option):** the side panel shows who you are talking to (picture for their current mood, what they are doing, what they are wearing) in place of your avatar, with a small button to flip back.
- **Automatic background (option):** after each reply the helper AI says where the scene is and the screen backdrop follows: a Places picture or an earlier background is reused, a new place gets a new one. **Dramatic-moment backgrounds (option)** draw a variant for an alarm, fire, power failure or battle and return to the plain one afterwards. Both switches are in 🖼 Art → Backgrounds.
- **Separate picture maker for backgrounds and places (option, ComfyUI):** for example FLUX.2 klein, which draws scenery well; includes a built-in workflow and a test button. Characters, scene pictures and covers keep the maker you chose.
- **Per-checkpoint picture settings (ComfyUI):** reference strength, where the reference stops applying, refine denoise, steps, extra words, whether to use the refine pass, how many references to use and whether to crop them to the face, each saved per checkpoint, so an anime and a realistic checkpoint can be tuned separately. The picture you chose for a character ("normal 1") is always the main reference.
- **Character picture tags:** for tag-style picture models the helper writes each character's look tags once; you can read and edit them (character page → Images → Picture tags) or have them rewritten, and they are used in every portrait and scene picture.
- **Extended mood set (option):** new characters get the extended set of mood pictures by default, and from the second turn on the narrator is reminded each turn to use the whole range instead of mostly normal and smile.
- **Ask for exact moods (option):** the narrator names a character's real feeling and uses a listed mood only when it is an exact match, so you are offered a new picture for each new mood.
- **Cover and avatar pictures: pick from 4.** The cover editor shows the picture description (edit it, or have it written from the story), then "Draw 4 options"; avatars work the same way. Unchosen options are removed after a day.
- **Other names for characters:** an "Also called" box (so a nickname counts as the same character), a merge for duplicates, and a "Names and forms of address" box (who may use which name for them) that is sent to the narrator every turn.
- **Two-tone story text (Settings → Appearance):** dim narration, bright dialogue, with an adjustable dimness.
- **Usage → Narrator cache:** how many replies read the prompt from Claude's cache and, for the rest, why it had to be written again (story prompt or settings changed, summary changed, earlier messages changed, cache expired, first turn since start).
- **Collapsible sections** in Settings, Art → Style & options and Art → Picture makers.

**Improved**
- **Cheaper picture and helper jobs.** Mood portraits for tag-style models use the character's saved tags plus built-in mood tags (no model call for the standard moods); scene pictures ask the model only for expressions, action, setting and framing; the new-character check runs only when a new name appears; the side panel reads values the narrator already prints ("Health: 80   Gold: 12") without the AI.
